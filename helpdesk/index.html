IRL Pro to OBS Setup

A streamlined guide to getting your Android camera feed into OBS using an SRT connection with no external servers.

Local Network (At Home)

Follow these steps when your phone and streaming PC are on the exact same WiFi network. ⚠️ Keep in mind you will not be able to use bonding on your local network.

1. Get Your PC's IP Address

  1. Press Win + R, type cmd, and hit Enter.
  2. Type ipconfig and press Enter.
  3. Locate your IPv4 Address (usually looks like 192.168.1.X) and write it down.

2. Set Up OBS to Receive Video

  1. In OBS, add a new Media Source to your scene and name it "Phone Camera".
  2. Uncheck the box for "Local File".
  3. In the Input box, paste exactly: srt://0.0.0.0:7654?mode=listener
  4. In the Input Format box, type: mpegts
  5. Uncheck "Restart playback when source becomes active", and click OK.

3. Connect the IRL Pro App

  1. Open IRL Pro on your Android phone (ensure you are on your home WiFi).
  2. Go to Settings > Connections.
  3. Under SRT Setup, enter this URL: srt://[YOUR_LOCAL_IP]:7654 (Replace [YOUR_LOCAL_IP] with the address from Step 1).
  4. Save and go to the main camera screen. Your video should now appear in OBS!

Phase 2: Mobile Streaming (Outside)

To stream from outside your house using 4G/5G, you must allow the video feed through your home internet router.

Important: SRT uses the UDP protocol. Ensure you select UDP when setting up your port forwarding rules.

1. Port Forward on Your Router

  1. Log into your router's admin settings using your web browser.
  2. Find the Port Forwarding section.
  3. Create a new rule: Forward Port 7654 using Protocol UDP to your OBS PC's Local IP address (from Phase 1).
  4. Save and apply the router settings.

2. Connect Using Your Public IP

  1. On your OBS PC, visit a site like whatismyip.com to find your Public IPv4 Address.
  2. Turn off WiFi on your phone so you are only using Mobile Data.
  3. In the IRL Pro app's SRT Setup, change the URL to: srt://[YOUR_PUBLIC_IP]:7654
  4. Save settings. Your phone is now securely streaming via mobile data to your home PC.
